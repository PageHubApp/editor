---
title: Getting ready for the first frost
date: 2026-09-28
summary: Cover the tender crops, pick the last tomatoes and plant the garlic.
---

The forecast says frost by the end of next week. Pick every tomato that has started to blush; they will ripen on a windowsill.

Cover peppers and basil with the old bedsheets from the shed, and take the covers off in the morning so the plants can breathe.

Garlic goes in now. Plant cloves pointy side up, about four inches deep, and mulch with straw.
