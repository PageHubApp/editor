---
title: The great tomato trial
date: 2026-07-02
summary: Six varieties, six beds, and one clear winner by the end of July.
---

Six of us each grew a different variety in the same kind of bed and kept notes. The early cherry tomatoes won on flavor and on how little attention they needed.

The big heirlooms cracked after the July storm, which is why the jar of seeds on the shed table is mostly cherry this year.
