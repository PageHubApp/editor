---
title: Two new rain barrels
date: 2026-08-14
summary: The shed roof now fills two 200 litre barrels, enough to water the north beds all week.
---

Thanks to everyone who came to the work morning. We connected the shed gutters to two new barrels and added a tap at a height a watering can fits under.

Please use the barrels first and the hose second. A full barrel is about four watering rounds for the north beds.
