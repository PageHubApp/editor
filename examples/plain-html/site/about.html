About the studio

Tidewater Pottery started in a garage in 2014 and moved to the harbor in 2019.

Our story

Mara Ellison threw her first bowl at a community class and never stopped. Today she and two apprentices make everything sold here, from the clay body to the final glaze.

We fire in a small electric kiln twice a week, so each batch is small and every piece is looked at before it leaves.

The studio in numbers

  • 3 potters
  • 2 firings a week
  • 1,400 pieces last year

Classes

Saturday morning wheel classes run in six week blocks. Everything is included, and you take your finished pieces home.
