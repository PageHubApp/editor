Good to know

Can I order a custom set?

Yes. Custom sets take about six weeks. Tell us the colors and how many place settings you need.

Are the glazes food safe?

Every glaze we use is food safe, dishwasher safe and microwave safe.

Do you ship?

We ship across the country. Pieces are wrapped in recycled paper and double boxed.

Visit the studio

Drop in Thursday to Sunday between 10 and 5. Wheel throwing demos run at noon on Saturdays.
